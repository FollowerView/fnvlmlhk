## Main Features

### Sapphire 2026
- **3000+ VFX Presets** — Hollywood-grade visual effects including lens flares, light leaks, glows, blurs, and stylized transitions used in major films
- **LensFlare Designer** — Build custom lens flares with physically accurate optical elements, anamorphic distortion, and chromatic aberration
- **Effect & Transition Builder** — Create custom effects and transitions with node-based workflow and save as reusable presets
- **GPU Acceleration** — CUDA and Metal acceleration for real-time preview and faster render times on complex effect stacks
- **Mocha Tracking Integration** — Advanced planar tracking and masking powered by Mocha directly inside Sapphire effects

### Continuum 2026
- **250+ Creative Plugins** — Comprehensive plugin suite including color correction, keying, compositing, 3D objects, particles, and image restoration
- **Primatte Studio Keying** — Professional chroma keying with automatic spill suppression and edge refinement for clean greenscreen composites
- **Title Studio 3D** — Full 3D title animation with extruded text, animated materials, particles, and broadcast-quality templates
- **Particle Illusion** — Powerful particle system with 6000+ presets for fire, smoke, explosions, magic effects, and motion graphics
- **BCC Lens Correction** — Fix lens distortion, chromatic aberration, and vignetting with automatic camera profile detection

### Mocha Pro 2026
- **Planar Motion Tracking** — Industry-standard planar tracking for challenging shots with motion blur, occlusions, and camera shake
- **Advanced Rotoscoping** — AI-assisted roto tools with edge snapping, automatic shape propagation, and onion skinning
- **Remove Module** — Object removal and cleanup using temporal frame analysis for seamless paint-out of unwanted elements
- **Insert Module** — Screen replacement and match moving with perspective-correct compositing for VFX integration
- **Stabilization & Deshaker** — Smooth shaky footage or lock specific objects while maintaining natural camera movement

### Optics 2026
- **Photorealistic Lighting** — Add realistic lens flares, light leaks, bokeh, chromatic aberration, and optical effects based on real camera lenses
- **Lens Emulation Library** — Over 170 lens and filter presets emulating Panavision, Cooke, Zeiss, and vintage cinema glass
- **Photoshop Integration** — Works as Photoshop plugin for still image compositing with full layer and adjustment layer support
- **Custom Filter Designer** — Build unique optical effects by stacking multiple lens elements with adjustable intensity and blending

## Pro Tips

- **Enable GPU Acceleration** — Check Preferences → Video Rendering to use GPU for real-time Sapphire and Continuum effects
- **Use Mocha Tracking First** — Track difficult shots in standalone Mocha Pro, then copy tracking data to After Effects for final composite
- **Save Custom Presets** — Build reusable effect presets in Sapphire's Effect Builder and organize in project-specific libraries
- **Pre-render Heavy Effects** — Pre-comp and pre-render complex particle simulations to maintain smooth timeline playback during editing
- **Update Plugin Paths** — If effects don't appear after installation, manually point host app to plugin folder in preferences
